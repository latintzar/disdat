The .hero is overflowing its vertical rhythm — padding-block is 24px under a 96px display heading, so raise it to 64px; and the .cta sits at 92px wide with a 2px radius while its card uses 14px, so set it width:100% and match the radius.

React to dis. Skillify dat.

you’d rather just react

You can see it’s wrong — you just can’t name it.

The spacing’s wrong, the button feels cheap. You don’t know the property, the value, or the selector to say so — and you shouldn’t have to. React in plain words; your agent gets the exact term.

Iterate on the bit that feels offPoint at a live bug
you’d rather learn from the best

See something you love? Skillify it.

Point at any page you admire and Dis Dat hands your agent its real layers — then the skill turns them into knobs it has mastered. You don’t get a knockoff of their site; your agent gains the skill of the developer behind it, and builds at that level in your brand.

Hi — I’m Peter.

I made Dis Dat to give coding agents the page context I was pointing at. The extension captures what I mean while I talk, then saves a file my agent can read.

Before this I pitched Selva — an airborne-DNA biodiversity sampler for farms — to Jony Ive through King Charles’s Terra Carta prize, and presented it at the Royal Society. RCA (MA, Design Products) · UCL (BEng). I cut my teeth on London’s wholesale food markets and on gnarly logistics at Gojek.
